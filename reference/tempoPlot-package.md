# tempoPlot: Microbiome Time Series Visualization

Plots longitudinal microbiome data stored in (Tree)SummarizedExperiment
and MultiAssayExperiment objects: sampling timelines, trajectories of
subjects and features, ordination paths and community composition over
time. Plots use results computed with miaTime and mia and are returned
as ggplot2 objects.
